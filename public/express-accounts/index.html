How it works

1

Sign in securely

Create a free account using your Google or Apple ID — no separate password needed.

2

Capture or import receipts

Photograph a receipt or upload a bank PDF. The app reads the date, amount, and merchant automatically.

3

Review & categorise

Check the extracted details, tweak the category if needed, and save. It takes seconds.

4

Analyse your spending

Open the Summary tab for a breakdown of monthly spend by category, with VAT estimates where applicable.
